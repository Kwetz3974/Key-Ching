# 🔑 Key-Ching

### Learn. Simulate. Test. Earn.

**Key-Ching is a learning website that teaches beginners how to keep their Bitcoin wallets safe and how to get it back if they lose their phone.**


## 🎯 The Problem

Losing access to a phone should not mean losing access to your Bitcoin.

Many people who start using Bitcoin never learn how to back up their wallet or recover it. They often find out why this matters only after something goes wrong.

## 💡 Idea

Key-Ching lets beginners practise before a real emergency happens. Instead of reading long, confusing guides, users learn through short lessons, practice scenarios and quizzes and are rewarded for learning.

## ✨ What You Can Do in Key-Ching

- **Learn:** Short, simple lessons on keeping Bitcoin safe and recovering it.
- **Simulate:** Practice with made-up situations, such as losing a phone, and see what to do.
- **Test:** Take quizzes to check what you have learned.
- **Earn:** Pass a quiz to see how a Bitcoin reward could work.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. A user reads a lesson.
2. They try a practice scenario.
3. They take a quiz.
4. If they pass, they get a reward.

## ⚡ About the Rewards

The rewards in this version are a **demonstration**. No real Bitcoin is sent. They show how Bitcoin could be used to encourage people to learn, using Flash as the payment partner. Sending real rewards is a future step.

## 🔐 Safe by Design

- Key-Ching never asks for your real Bitcoin passwords or backup words.
- Nobody can lose real money using this app.

## 🛣️ What Comes Next

This is an early version built for a hackathon. Next steps we would like to take:

- Send real Bitcoin rewards.
- Let users create accounts and track their progress.
- Add more lessons for people who want to go deeper.
- Make rewards harder to cheat, so they go to people who truly learn.
